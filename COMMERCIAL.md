## Do I need a commercial license?

| Your situation | License |
|---|---|
| Private / personal use | AGPL — free |
| Use inside your company, unmodified | AGPL — free |
| You modify Brainy and only use it internally (nobody outside your organisation interacts with it) | AGPL — free |
| You modify Brainy and let **others** use it over a network (SaaS, customer portal, hosted service) and do **not** want to publish your changes | **Commercial** |
| You embed Brainy in a closed-source product you sell or distribute | **Commercial** |
| Your legal department does not allow AGPL software | **Commercial** |

The commercial license removes the AGPL obligations (publishing source code of modifications and
of network-facing deployments) and can include support and priority fixes.
